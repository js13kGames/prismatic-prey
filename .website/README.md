**The last unicorn is being hunted. Use rainbow magic to fight back.**

Prismatic Prey is a dark, casual survival game. Guide a lone white unicorn through an endless monochrome forest while a growing human mob closes in. There is no escape. Survive longer and score higher.

## Collect the rainbow

Colored flowers are the only bright things left in the forest. Each grants a temporary auto-attacking weapon:

- **Red Lance** pierces enemies in a line.
- **Orange Orbit** burns anything that gets too close.
- **Yellow Chain** leaps between clustered targets.
- **Poison Mist** damages enemies across a wide area.
- **Blue Pulse** blasts the mob away.
- **Indigo Gravity** pulls distant enemies inward.
- **Violet Seeker** hunts nearby targets.

Collect the same color again to strengthen its effect and refresh its remaining time up to the maximum duration. Keep all seven colors active at once to unleash the screen-clearing **Prismatic Stampede**.

## Survive the hunt

Defeated enemies leave faint rainbow XP. Gather it to level up and strengthen every flower weapon. The mob thickens with time, and contact with the enemies can drain HP quickly.

Dash through openings, manage regenerating stamina, and use each temporary weapon at the right moment. HP regenerates slowly, so every mistake matters.

Play with keyboard, mouse, or gamepad. Control hints switch automatically when you change input method.

## Controls

- **Keyboard:** `WASD` or arrows move, `Space` dashes, `Escape` pauses
- **Mouse:** left-click a destination, hold left to steer, right-click to dash
- **Gamepad:** left stick or D-pad moves, `A` dashes or selects, `Menu` pauses

Built for **js13kGames 2026: Unicorns and Rainbows**. Prismatic Prey uses a 320x200 DOS-inspired view, an infinite deterministic forest, layered DOM sprites, and canvas particles. Rainbow magic and blood are the only color in the darkness.
